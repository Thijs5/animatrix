# Omarchy O / Quiet Glow

The actual stepped O from Omarchy's wordmark, centred on the GA402 LED area. The letter stays at a steady brightness. A narrow halo around its inner and outer boundaries breathes over 24 seconds. Halo peak brightness varies between 8 and 38 out of 255; the letter remains at 228. The dark opening stays distinct.

The mathematical loop endpoint matches exactly, and the last exported sample differs from the first by at most one brightness level. Sixteen subpixel samples preserve the stepped silhouette.

- `omarchy.gif`: installed display artwork, 74 × 39, 10 fps, infinite loop.
- `omarchy.png`: static display with halo at its brightest.
- `preview.gif` / `preview.png`: physical dot-layout previews.
- `drawing.svg`: editable letter silhouette; halo is generated by the distance field.

Generate: `uv run --with numpy --with pillow python artwork/omarchy-glow/generate.py`.

Use **As is** layout, original frame rate, restart looping, no rotation or tilt compensation. Installed as **Omarchy Glow**, retaining the other profiles and existing power/lid/global brightness settings. Reference: `/usr/share/omarchy/logo.svg`, read without modification.
